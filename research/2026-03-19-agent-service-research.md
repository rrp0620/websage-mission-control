## What Actually Works (Reddit lesson)
- **Build around existing habits, not against them** — if they live in WhatsApp, build a WhatsApp bot. Don't make them adopt new dashboards.
- **Map their ACTUAL workflow** before building (2-3 days of understanding)
- Integration beats innovation. Plug into what they already use.
- 80% of first automations sat unused because they didn't fit workflow
