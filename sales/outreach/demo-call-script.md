# Demo Call Script — Real Estate Lead Bot
## For: Rishi | Length: 10–15 min

---

## BEFORE THE CALL
- Have your phone ready with the demo number saved as "Sarah - AI Demo"
- Have this page open for reference
- Know their name, brokerage, city (check LinkedIn before)

---

## THE CALL

### Open (1 min) — Make it about them, not you
> "Hey [Name], thanks for taking the time. Before I show you anything — tell me, what does your lead follow-up look like right now? Like when a Zillow lead comes in at 10pm, what actually happens?"

*Shut up. Let them talk. This is gold.*

Listen for:
- "I try to respond quickly but..." → pain
- "We have a CRM that..." → objection incoming, note it
- "Honestly, I don't always get to them right away" → PERFECT, you have them

---

### The Setup (1 min) — Reframe before demo
> "So the research shows 78% of buyers go with the first agent who responds. And the average response time industry-wide is over 11 hours. What we built is an agent that responds in under 60 seconds — at 3am if that's when the lead comes in."

---

### The Live Demo (4 min) — THIS IS THE CLOSE

> "Let me just show you instead of telling you. Text this number right now: [DEMO NUMBER]. Pretend you're a lead — just say something like 'hey I saw a house on Zillow I'm interested in'."

*Have them text it from their own phone. Watch their face.*

Sarah will respond within ~5 seconds. Walk them through:
- "See how she responds immediately — that's what your leads get at any hour"
- "Watch — she's going to ask one question at a time. Not a form, not a list."
- Keep texting back with them. Get to the booking step.
- "And right there — she just offered your calendar link. That's an appointment on your books, zero effort."

---

### The Customization Pitch (2 min)
> "This is a generic demo. Yours would say [Their Name] and [Their Brokerage]. It would know your service area, your calendar, your phone number for warm handoffs. We set all that up — takes us about 2 days."

---

### Handle Their CRM Objection (if raised)
> "So [CRM] is great for managing leads you're already working. This is upstream from that — it's catching the leads before they go cold and getting them qualified before they ever hit your CRM. They actually work well together."

---

### The Ask (2 min) — Don't be weird, just ask
> "So — based on what you just saw, does this solve a real problem for you?"

*Wait for yes.*

> "Great. Here's how it works: we do a $500 setup — that's us configuring it with your branding, your calendar, testing it — and then $350/month to keep it running, updated, and supported. We can have you live by [day after tomorrow]."

If they hesitate:
> "I'll tell you what — let's do a 30-day pilot. If it doesn't book you at least 2 qualified calls in the first month, I'll refund the setup fee. Zero risk."

---

### Close
> "Want to get started? I just need a few quick details from you — your calendar link, your brokerage name, and the lead sources you use. We can do that right now in 5 minutes."

Collect:
- [ ] Full name + brokerage name
- [ ] Service area / city
- [ ] Calendar link (Calendly, Google Cal, etc.)
- [ ] Their cell number (for warm handoffs and their own test)
- [ ] Lead sources (Zillow, website, Realtor.com, etc.)
- [ ] Payment (Stripe link — we'll set this up)

---

## IF THEY SAY NOT YET

> "Totally fine. What's the main hesitation — is it timing, budget, or you just want to think it over?"

Listen, address, then:
> "I'll send you a quick video recap so you have it. And I'll check back in [1 week] — by then you'll probably have had a lead go cold and it'll feel more urgent." (say this lightly, with a smile)

Send follow-up within 1 hour: short Loom video or voice note recapping what you showed them.

---

## POST-CALL CHECKLIST
- [ ] Log outcome in prospect spreadsheet
- [ ] If closed: send onboarding details to Mint to start setup
- [ ] If not yet: schedule follow-up in calendar
- [ ] Note any objections you heard (helps us improve)
