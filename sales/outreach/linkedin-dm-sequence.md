# LinkedIn DM Sequence — Real Estate Agents
## Websage AI | Agent-as-a-Service

---

## Who to Target

**LinkedIn Search:**
- Title: "Real Estate Agent" OR "Realtor" OR "Real Estate Broker"
- Location: [Your city / metro] — start local, easier to close
- Filter: 2nd connections (warm enough, not total strangers)
- Bonus signals to prioritize:
  - Active poster (posted in last 30 days)
  - Profile mentions Zillow, Realtor.com, or "leads"
  - Solo agent or small team (not a big brokerage machine)
  - Has a personal brand (headshot, bio, posts about their work)

**Volume target:** 25–30 connection requests + DMs per day (LinkedIn's soft limit)

## How to Handle Replies

**"How does it work?"**
> It's an AI agent that texts your leads the second they come in — like a 24/7 assistant. It asks qualifying questions (timeline, pre-approved, budget), then books a call directly on your calendar when they're ready. You only get involved when the lead is warm.
>
> Want me to show you a live demo? Takes 10 min over a quick call.

**"How much does it cost?"**
> Setup is $500, then $350/month. One extra closing pays for it for years.
>
> We're also offering 3 pilot spots at $250/$250 right now while we collect early case studies. Want to grab one of those?

**"I already have a follow-up system"**
> Totally fair — most CRMs do some version of this. The difference is this one has an actual conversation with leads. Not a generic email drip — it texts them, answers questions, and qualifies them like a human assistant would.
>
> Takes 10 min to show the difference. Worth a look?

**"Not interested"**
> No worries at all — appreciate you responding! If that ever changes, feel free to reach out. Good luck out there 🏡

## Pro Tips

- **Personalize the opener** — mention their city, a listing they posted, or their brokerage. 2 seconds of research = 2x reply rate.
- **Send DMs Tuesday–Thursday, 8–10am or 5–7pm** — highest open rates
- **Don't pitch in the first message** — ask a question, create curiosity
- **Voice notes on LinkedIn** = 3x reply rate (record a 20-second personal intro)
- **Comment on their posts first** — then DM. They'll recognize your name.
