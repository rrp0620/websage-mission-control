# LinkedIn DM Sequences — Real Estate Agents
## Websage AI · Agent-as-a-Service

---

## How to Find Prospects on LinkedIn

Search: `real estate agent [city]`
Filter: People → 2nd connections → Sort by "Recently Active"
Cities to start: Austin TX, Dallas TX, Phoenix AZ, Charlotte NC, Nashville TN

Target profile:
- Solo agent or small team (not big brokerages — they have IT depts)
- Active on LinkedIn (posted in last 30 days)
- Has Zillow/Realtor.com profile (means they're paying for leads already)
- 2–15 years experience (hungry but established enough to spend $300/mo)

---

## DM Sequence (5 touches over 10 days)

### Touch 1 — Day 1 (Connection Request Note)
> "Hey [Name] — love what you're doing in [city]. I work with real estate agents on AI tools that automate lead follow-up. Would love to connect."

---

### Touch 2 — Day 1-2 (After they accept — Opening DM)

**Version A — Direct:**
> "Hey [Name], thanks for connecting!
> 
> Quick question — when a lead comes in from Zillow at 9pm, what happens? Do you text them back that night or follow up in the morning?
> 
> Asking because I built something that handles that automatically. Happy to share more if it sounds interesting."

**Version B — Pain-first:**
> "Hey [Name]! Appreciate the connect.
>
> I've been talking to a lot of agents lately and the #1 thing I keep hearing is: 'I lose deals because I don't follow up fast enough.'
>
> Built an AI agent that texts new leads in under 60 seconds, qualifies them, and books calls — even at midnight. Working really well for a few agents. Worth a quick look?"

**Version C — Curiosity:**
> "Thanks for connecting, [Name]!
>
> Random question: if you could only fix ONE thing about your lead follow-up process, what would it be?
>
> (Building something in that space and want to make sure I'm solving the right problems)"

→ Use Version C most — it starts a conversation, feels human, and gets them talking.

---

### Touch 3 — Day 4-5 (If no reply to Touch 2)
> "Hey [Name] — just wanted to bump this up in case it got buried.
>
> Short version: I built an AI text agent for real estate agents that responds to Zillow/website leads instantly, qualifies them, and drops warm leads in your lap.
>
> Happy to send a 60-second demo video if you're curious. No pitch, just want to see if it's useful."

---

### Touch 4 — Day 7-8 (If still no reply — value drop)
> "Last thing from me [Name] — sharing this in case it's useful regardless:
>
> 78% of buyers go with the first agent to respond. Average response time in the industry is 11 hours.
>
> The agents winning right now are the ones responding in under 2 minutes. That's what I help with. 
>
> If timing is ever right, happy to chat: [calendar link]"

---

### Touch 5 — Day 10 (Final — breakup)
> "Hey [Name] — going to stop following up after this, promise 😄
>
> If the idea of having an AI handle your lead follow-up while you sleep ever sounds interesting, just reply 'yes' and I'll send you a demo.
>
> Either way, best of luck in [city] — you're doing great things."

## When They Reply (Common Scenarios)

**"How does it work?"**
> "It's an AI agent that texts your new leads automatically — from Zillow, your website, wherever. It introduces itself, asks qualifying questions (timeline, pre-approved, budget), and either books a call on your calendar or flags you when they're warm.
>
> Best way to see it is a quick demo — got 10 minutes this week?"

**"How much does it cost?"**
> "Setup is $500, then $350/month after that. Most agents I work with close 1-2 extra deals/year just from leads they would've missed — that covers the cost many times over.
>
> Want to see it in action first before talking numbers?"

**"I already have a CRM"**
> "Totally — most agents do. The difference is this actually has a real conversation via text, not just an automated email sequence. It asks follow-up questions, handles objections, and only pings you when the lead is warm.
>
> Happy to show you side-by-side if that'd be helpful."

**"Send me more info"**
> "Sure! Here's a 60-second demo: [video link — we'll record this]
>
> And here's a quick booking link if you want to chat live: [calendar link]
>
> Let me know what you think!"
