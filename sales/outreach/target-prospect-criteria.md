# Target Prospect Criteria — Real Estate Agents

## Ideal Prospect Profile

### Green lights (prioritize these)
- Solo agent or small team (1–5 people) — decision maker is the person you're talking to
- Active on Zillow / Realtor.com — they have inbound lead flow to automate
- Has a personal website — signals they invest in their business
- Posts on social media about their work — they're trying to grow
- 2–10 years in the business — established enough to have leads, hungry enough to want more
- Located in a mid-to-large metro (more lead volume = more pain)

### Yellow lights (can still work, requires more tailoring)
- Brand new agent — may not have enough lead volume yet
- Part of a large brokerage with its own tech stack — may face internal resistance
- Residential + commercial mixed — slightly more complex needs

### Red lights (skip)
- Property management only — different workflow
- Commercial-only — longer sales cycle, different pain
- Already has a full ISA (inside sales agent) team — they've solved the problem

## Where to Find Them

1. **LinkedIn** — Primary channel. Search by title + location.
2. **Google Maps** — "real estate agent [city]" → scrape names + websites → find LinkedIn/email
3. **Zillow Agent Directory** — zillow.com/agent-finder — browse by city, filter by reviews
4. **Realtor.com Agent Directory** — same concept
5. **Local Facebook Groups** — "[City] Real Estate Professionals" groups — warm leads

---

## Quick Scrape Method (for cold email)
1. Go to Google Maps → search "real estate agent Austin TX"
2. Open each listing → grab: name, phone, website, email if listed
3. Check their website for a contact email
4. Add to outreach tracker
5. Target: 50 emails/week to start

Tool-free method works fine at this scale. No paid tools needed yet.
