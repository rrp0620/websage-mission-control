# Websage AI — Outreach & Sales Playbook
## Agent-as-a-Service: Real Estate Lead Bot

---

## The Pitch (in one sentence)
"We build an AI agent that texts your new leads within 60 seconds, qualifies them, and books calls on your calendar — while you sleep."

---

## Target Profile
- **Who:** Real estate agents, solo or small team (1–5 agents)
- **Where:** Local markets. Austin, Dallas, Phoenix, Charlotte — mid-size metros work well
- **Pain:** Getting Zillow/website leads and responding too slow. Everyone loses deals this way.
- **Tech comfort:** Low-medium. They use their phone all day. Won't adopt new software.
- **Budget:** $300–$800/month is nothing if it books 1 extra closing/year

## The Demo Call (Rishi leads, I prep materials)

**Goal:** Show them the bot in action, not slides.

**Flow:**
1. (2 min) "Can I ask — what happens right now when a Zillow lead comes in at 9pm on a Sunday?"
   → Let them describe the pain. They'll sell themselves.
2. (3 min) Pull up the demo on your phone. Text the demo number. Show them Sarah responding in real time.
3. (3 min) Show the qualification flow — how it books a call, notifies the agent.
4. (2 min) "We set this up for you in about 2 days. $500 to build and configure it for your brand, then $350/month to keep it running — includes updates and support."

**Key objection handling:**

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I already have a CRM that does follow-up" | "Does it text leads in under 60 seconds and have an actual conversation? Most CRMs send a generic email. This is different — want to see?" |
| "What if the bot says something wrong?" | "You approve the script. It only answers what we program it to know. Anything outside that, it hands off to you." |
| "I need to think about it" | "Totally fair. I'll send you a 2-min video of it in action. If you want to pilot it for a month and cancel if it doesn't book a single call — I'll refund you." |
| "It's too expensive" | "One extra closing pays for this for 3 years. But if budget is tight, we can start with just the SMS bot at $250/month." |

## Onboarding (what happens after they say yes)

Day 0: Collect: agent name, phone #, brokerage name, service area, calendar link, lead sources
Day 1: Configure agent, set up Twilio number, test internally
Day 2: Go live, send test lead together on the call
Week 1: Check in, adjust bot responses based on real conversations
Month 1: Report: X leads handled, X qualified, X calls booked
