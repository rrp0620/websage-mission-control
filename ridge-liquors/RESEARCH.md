## Location & Demographics

- **Claymont, DE** is a community in New Castle County, just south of the Pennsylvania border — a prime spot for cross-border shoppers from Pennsylvania and Philadelphia suburbs.
- The area has a notable South Asian (Indian) population, which influences product demand (imported spirits, international beer brands, etc.).
- Delaware has **no state sales tax**, which is a significant draw for cross-border customers from PA and NJ.
- Delaware reportedly has **more liquor stores per capita than any other U.S. state**, making it a competitive but high-traffic market.

## Opportunities & Notes

- **No website** is a gap — a basic site with hours, product highlights, and delivery info could drive organic traffic.
- Cross-border PA/NJ shoppers are a natural target audience — no sales tax is a strong differentiator worth marketing.
- Delivery service is offered but not prominently marketed — worth promoting on Google Business, Yelp, etc.
- Strong competition from Total Wine means Ridge Liquors should lean into **community feel, personalized service, and niche/local selection** as differentiators.
- International/Indian spirits segment could be a niche worth doubling down on given local demographics.
- Google Business Profile should be claimed/optimized if not already done.
