## Catering Target Segments (Newark, DE)

### 1. 🎓 University of Delaware
- **Departments & Admin Offices** — meetings, faculty events, orientation days
- **Athletics Department** — team recovery smoothies post-practice (NBA-compliant ingredients is a selling point)
- **Student Organizations / Greek Life** — events, health fairs
- **Campus Rec Center** — wellness programming partnerships
- **Contact strategy:** Email department admins and student affairs coordinators directly. Offer a sample catering box.

### 2. 🏢 Corporate Offices (Greater Newark/Wilmington Corridor)
- **AstraZeneca** (Newark campus) — pharma culture = health-conscious employees
- **DuPont** (nearby)
- **JP Morgan Chase** (nearby)
- Any office with 20+ employees is a potential weekly catering client
- **Contact strategy:** LinkedIn outreach to office managers and executive assistants. Offer a "lunch & learn" smoothie tasting.

### 3. 💪 Fitness Studios & Gyms
- **Anytime Fitness Newark** (South Main location)
- Local CrossFit boxes, yoga studios, personal training gyms
- Partner for post-class smoothie packages or member discounts that lead to bulk orders
- **Contact strategy:** Walk in, speak to the owner/manager, offer a trial catering package for their next event.

### 4. 🏃 Running Clubs & Sports Teams
- Local adult rec leagues, youth sports organizations
- Tournament day smoothie packages
- **Contact strategy:** Facebook groups, local Parks & Rec department outreach

### 5. 🏥 Healthcare & Wellness
- Medical offices, urgent care centers, physical therapy clinics
- Staff appreciation events, healthy catering alternative to pizza
- **Contact strategy:** Direct mail flyer + follow-up call to office managers
