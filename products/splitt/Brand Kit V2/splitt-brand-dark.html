The correct value framing
Splitt. does not "save users money." Users with a $25 stipend will spend that $25 regardless. The real value: (1) eliminating the workaround tax — no more double delivery fees. (2) Rewards optimization — earn from multiple cards on the same purchase. (3) Benefit card freedom — spend FSA, HSA, and stipend balances anywhere, at any amount.
