Color usage rules
• Lime only for action elements — buttons, active states, the dot, success confirmations
• Cyan for the second card in split visualizations. Never as a primary CTA color.
• Off-white is the page background, not pure white. Always #f5f3ee.
• Black is #0a0a0a — not pure #000000. The difference matters.

The correct value framing
Splitt. does not "save users money." Users with a $25 stipend will spend that $25 regardless. The real value: (1) eliminating the workaround tax — no more double delivery fees to use your stipend. (2) Rewards optimization — earn from multiple cards on the same purchase, for the first time. (3) Benefit card freedom — spend FSA, HSA, and stipend balances anywhere, at any amount.
