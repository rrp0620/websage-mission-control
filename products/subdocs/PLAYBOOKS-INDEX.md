## HOW THESE PLAYBOOKS FIT TOGETHER

```

1. AWARENESS
   → Sees your LinkedIn post (content-calendar.md)

2. ENGAGEMENT
   → Connects on LinkedIn / comments / replies
   → You respond with outreach template (outreach-templates.md)

3. DEMO REQUEST
   → They ask for demo or free trial signup

4. CONVERSION
   → You use demo script (sales-playbook.md)
   → You handle objections (sales-playbook.md)
   → They upgrade (email-sequences.md triggers)

5. RETENTION
   → They ask support questions (faq-content.md)
   → You send nurture emails (email-sequences.md)
   → You blog about their pain (content-calendar.md)
```

## EDITING & CUSTOMIZATION

All templates are copy-paste ready, but personalize:

### In Email Sequences
- Replace [First Name] with actual name
- Replace [Company] with actual company
- Replace [X] days with actual number (e.g., "Day 14 no login" = they've been inactive)

### In Outreach Templates
- Replace [Name] with actual first name
- Replace [city] / [region] with actual location
- Replace [trade] with their trade (roofing, HVAC, solar, etc.)
- Keep the sentiment and structure — just update specific details

### In Sales Playbook
- Demo script is word-for-word ready
- Objection handlers: use the script as-is, then add your own detail/story
- Sales plays: follow the flow, add your personality

### In FAQ
- No edits needed before posting
- Add links to blog articles (fill in actual URLs when live)
- Add your company name and Rishi's email where indicated

### In Content Calendar
- Titles are suggestions — adjust based on what's happening
- Week 1-4 are detailed; weeks 5-12 are themes — create your own posts within those themes
- Blog outlines are starting points — expand/contract based on what you want to cover

## IMPORTANT NOTES

1. **This is the Closer's domain.** You own sales, outreach, and conversion. Marketer owns content.
2. **These are launch documents.** They're built for Month 1-3 acquisition. In Month 4+, you'll evolve them based on what works.
3. **Every template is GC language.** Never say "vendor," "compliance," "leverage," "utilize." Say "sub," "COI," "audit."
4. **Consistency beats perfection.** Post every week, respond to every comment, send a few cold emails daily. 3 months of consistency > 2 weeks of perfection.
5. **Track what works.** After 2 weeks, you'll see patterns. Which LinkedIn posts get engagement? Which cold email subject line gets the most opens? Do more of those.
6. **Founder-led = trustworthy.** Sign emails as Rishi. Respond personally. GCs buy from people they trust, not brands.
