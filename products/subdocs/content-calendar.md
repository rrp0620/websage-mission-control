## CONTENT CREATION CHECKLIST

### Before Publishing Any Post

**LinkedIn:**
- [ ] Hook is in first 1-2 words (curiosity or fear or value)
- [ ] Under 150 words (usually)
- [ ] Call-to-action is clear (question, poll, or ask)
- [ ] Language is GC language (sub, COI, audit, not vendor/compliance)
- [ ] No emojis unless they fit the tone (use sparingly)
- [ ] Proofread (typos = untrustworthy)
- [ ] Schedule 24 hours ahead

**Facebook:**
- [ ] Community-first tone (asking, not telling)
- [ ] Under 200 words
- [ ] Encourages comments/discussion
- [ ] GC language
- [ ] Authentic voice (like talking to a peer)
- [ ] No salesy vibe (will get deleted by moderators)

**Blog:**
- [ ] Title has keyword (SEO)
- [ ] Outline is clear before writing
- [ ] Sections have headers
- [ ] Paragraphs are short (3-4 lines max)
- [ ] Includes real example or story
- [ ] CTA at the end (link to signup or free trial)
- [ ] Proofread by another person
- [ ] Include internal links to other relevant blog posts
- [ ] Add meta description (under 160 characters)

## NOTES

- **Consistency > Perfection.** Post regularly even if not perfect.
- **Engagement = Algorithm.** Respond to comments faster than the creator.
- **Your voice > Brand voice.** Sound like Rishi (a GC who gets it), not a marketing department.
- **Value first, sell later.** By the time someone asks "how do I fix this?", they're ready to hear about SubDocs.
- **Data > Assumptions.** Track what works. Do more of it. Kill what doesn't after 3 weeks.
