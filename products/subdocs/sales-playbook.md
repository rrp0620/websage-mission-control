# Sales Playbook — SubDocs
## Your guide to closing contractors who are tired of tracking COIs in spreadsheets

---

## SALES PROCESS OVERVIEW

SubDocs uses **Product-Led Growth + Founder-Led Assist** until we hit 100 paying customers.

### Flow
1. **Awareness** → Facebook Groups, LinkedIn, referral partners
2. **Signup** → Free tier (5-sub limit)
3. **Activation** → Add first sub, send upload link, see it work
4. **Conversion** → Hit 5-sub limit or 14+ days active → upsell email
5. **Assist** → If no conversion by day 21, Closer reaches out with demo/followup

**What triggers Closer outreach:**
- Inbound demo request
- High-intent lead from FB Group or LinkedIn
- Activation stalled (added subs but not sending links)
- Free tier expiring soon (month 2)

## ICP RECAP

**Who:** Owner-operator GC, small residential remodeling/HVAC/solar/roofing company

**Size:** 1-10 employees, managing 5-30 active subs

**Revenue:** $500K-$5M/year

**Where they work:** TX, FL, CA, GA, NC (priority states)

**What they fear:**
- An audit bill they didn't budget for ($6K-$8K surprise)
- EMR spike and 3-year premium increase
- Finding out a sub worked on a lapsed COI mid-year
- Losing sleep over compliance details

**What they use now:**
- Excel/Google Sheets to track COIs (unreliable)
- Email + calendar reminders (they forget)
- Jobber or HousecallPro (no COI module)
- Often: nothing systematic at all

**How they talk:**
- "COI" not "certificate of insurance"
- "sub" not "vendor"
- "audit" not "compliance review"
- "Workers' Comp" not "workers' compensation insurance"
- "EMR" (Experience Modification Rate)
- "lapsed policy" / "expired" / "non-compliant"

## OBJECTION HANDLERS

### Objection #1: "I already use spreadsheets"

**Their mindset:** "We're fine. It works."

**Your response:**
> "I hear that all the time. The problem with spreadsheets isn't the spreadsheet — it's that your subs have to remember to send you updates. And then you have to remember to follow up. One lapsed COI slips through during audit prep, and your insurance carrier is adding $6K to your audit bill. SubDocs flips that: your subs upload once, you get automatic alerts, and you catch expirations 30 days out. No following up. No surprises."

**Your ask:** "Can I show you the first 5 minutes? I'll show you what it looks like when a sub uploads a document and your dashboard automatically turns green."

**If they push back:** "What does your audit prep look like right now?"
→ *Listen.* Audit anxiety = your hook. Use it.

---

### Objection #2: "My insurance agent handles this"

**Their mindset:** "That's their job, not mine."

**Your response:**
> "Your agent can help you organize what you have, but SubDocs does something different: it collects documents automatically from your subs and tells you *before* the audit when something's about to expire. Your agent sees the problem at audit time. We catch it 30 days early. Your agent works *reactively*. We work *preventively*. And you own the data — it's yours, not theirs."

**Your ask:** "Ask your agent how many GCs they've seen get hit with surprise audit bills from lapsed COIs. They'll give you a number. Then ask if they have a way to *prevent* it. Spoiler: they don't. We do."

**If they stay skeptical:** "Most of our customers use an agent. SubDocs doesn't replace them — it just gives you peace of mind before the audit."

---

### Objection #3: "$29/month is too expensive"

**Their mindset:** "That's another line item on my P&L."

**Your response:**
> "I get it. But run this math with me: You have, what, 10 subs? One lapsed COI gets missed in an audit. Insurance carrier audits payroll, says that $40K in payments to that sub was uninsured. At construction trade rates, that's $6K to $8K in audit damages. SubDocs is $348 a year. One mistake pays for 17 to 23 years of the software. How much time do you spend right now chasing subs for updated COIs?"

**The closer:** "Compared to an audit surprise, $29 a month is free."

**If they're still hesitant:** "We have annual billing — it's $290 a year, saves you $58. Thinking about it that way?"

---

### Objection #4: "I only have a few subs"

**Their mindset:** "This tool is overkill for my size."

**Your response:**
> "Actually, the smaller you are, the more this matters. When you have 3 or 5 subs, you know them personally — so it feels manageable. But the audit doesn't care. One lapsed COI is one lapsed COI whether you have 3 subs or 30. And as you grow, you can't keep it all in your head. Better to build the habit now than scramble later. Plus, we have a free tier for up to 5 subs. Try it. If you never go above 5, you never pay."

**Your ask:** "When do you usually get your audit notice?"
→ *They'll tell you 60 days before.* "So you have one window a year to catch problems. Wouldn't you rather know 90 days out instead?"

---

### Objection #5: "I'll just set calendar reminders"

**Their mindset:** "I'm disciplined. I don't need automation."

**Your response:**
> "You probably are disciplined. But here's the thing: you have to set reminders 30, 14, and 7 days out for every single sub. Your office manager has to follow up. Your sub has to actually do it. A lot of failure points. What happens if you're on a job site and miss a reminder? The sub forgets to send it. You find out at audit. SubDocs removes the human error. Your sub gets a link, uploads it once, and you get automatic alerts. No discipline required. No memory required. Just done."

**Closer:** "You don't hire a project manager because you're undisciplined. You do it because humans forget things. SubDocs is your COI project manager."

## DEMO SCRIPT (15 MINUTES MAX)

**Objective:** Show the magic moment — add sub → send link → sub uploads → dashboard auto-updates

**Setup (2 min):**
"I'm going to walk you through SubDocs in three steps. First, I'll show you how you add a subcontractor. Then I'll show you the upload link your sub gets. Then I'll show you what your dashboard looks like when docs start coming in. Sound good?"

**Step 1: Add a Sub (2 min)**
- Login to dashboard
- Click "Add Subcontractor"
- Fill in: Name, company, trade
- Hit save
- "You just added a sub to your roster. Takes 30 seconds."

**Step 2: Send Upload Link (2 min)**
- Show sub detail page
- Click "Send Upload Link"
- Show the unique link (mobile-friendly)
- "Your sub gets this link in an email or text. No login required. No account to create. They just click and upload."

**Step 3: Simulate Sub Upload (3 min)**
- Open the upload link in a separate window (show mobile view)
- Walk through upload flow:
  - Select document type (COI)
  - Select file
  - Enter expiry date
  - Hit upload
- Flip back to dashboard
- "Watch the dashboard. Your sub's status just turned green because the COI is now on file and not expiring for [X] months."

**Step 4: Show Alerts/Automation (2 min)**
- Show the "Expiring Soon" section on dashboard
- "When a COI gets within 30 days of expiry, you get an email alert and your sub gets a reminder. No work on your part."

**Step 5: Pricing + Next Steps (2 min)**
- Show pricing
- Answer questions
- **Close:** "What would it be worth to never get surprised by a lapsed COI at audit time?"
  - Listen.
  - Then: "That's exactly why we built this. Want to get started?"

## QUALIFYING QUESTIONS (BEFORE DEMO)

Ask these to scope your time and set expectations:

1. **"How many subs are you managing right now?"**
   - <5 subs = good fit for free tier
   - 5-10 = ready to convert to Pro quickly
   - 10+ = immediate Pro fit + may need business tier

2. **"How are you currently tracking COIs?"**
   - Spreadsheet = easy value prop (automation)
   - Agent handles it = need to reframe (prevention vs. reaction)
   - Nothing = biggest pain + easiest close

3. **"When's your next audit coming up?"**
   - < 60 days = high urgency (show this in demo)
   - 60-120 days = medium urgency
   - 120+ days = plant the seed, nurture

4. **"Do you have anyone else who helps manage subs?"**
   - No = Pro plan features are nice-to-have
   - Yes = Business tier value (multi-user) is clearer

5. **"What's the biggest pain point with COI tracking right now?"**
   - Their own answer = your talking points in demo

## MESSAGING GUARDRAILS

### What to Say
- "Your subs upload once, you get automatic alerts"
- "Catch expirations 30 days early"
- "Peace of mind before your audit"
- "One lapsed COI = $6K-$8K audit bill"
- "SubDocs removes the follow-up"

### What NOT to Say
- "Compliance workflow" → say "tracking your subs' COIs"
- "Vendor" → say "sub"
- "Leverage" → just delete it
- "Utilize" → say "use"
- "Risk management" → say "audit protection" or "COI tracking"
- "Certificate of insurance" → they know it's a COI, just say COI
- "Regulatory" → say "audit"

### What Closer Should Know
- You are an authority on the GC pain. Own it.
- If you don't know the answer → "Let me find out and follow up by EOD"
- If they say "this won't work for us," ask *why* before defending
- If they're not interested after two attempts, let them go with: "If you ever hit a pain point with COIs, I'm here."

## RED FLAGS (Don't Spend Time Here)

- "We don't use subs" → not in ICP, move on

- They ask "do you have a phone support line?" → they're not tech-literate enough for SMB SaaS, probably not fit
