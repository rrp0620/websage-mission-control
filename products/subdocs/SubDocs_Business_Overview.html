The Problem
  • GCs must collect a Certificate of Insurance (COI) from every sub, every year
  • Most track COIs in Excel — subs let policies lapse, GCs don't notice
  • Annual audit arrives: carrier flags expired certs, charges GC as if the sub is an uninsured employee
  • Result: $6K–$14K+ surprise bill, insurance premium spike, rising EMR rate for 3 years
The Solution
  • GC adds their subs — SubDocs generates a personal upload link for each
  • Subs upload their own documents via a no-login mobile link
  • Dashboard shows Green / Yellow / Red compliance status for every sub
  • Auto alerts fire at 30, 14, and 7 days before any document expires
  • Audit arrives — GC pulls a one-click compliance report. Done.

The Sub Experience  (free — the network engine)

New Feature: Sub Accounts

When a sub submits their documents, SubDocs prompts them to save their email. This creates a free SubDocs account — a personal document wallet where all their compliance docs live in one place. SubDocs then reminds the sub before their own policy expires — so they never get caught off guard either. The sub can share their profile with any new GC instantly, instead of hunting down documents every time someone asks.

The audit math nobody told small GCs about

Workers' Comp class codes for construction trades run $15–$20 per $100 of payroll. If your sub's COI lapsed for even 3 months, the auditor applies that rate to everything you paid them. A framing crew paid $40K = a $6,000–$8,000 audit bill. The EMR rate spike then adds $4,800–$5,800 per year for the next 3 years. That's a $20,000+ event from one expired certificate.

Handling the Common Objections

"My subs won't upload their stuff."
They get a simple link — no account needed, works on any phone, takes 2 minutes. Free accounts give them a reason to use it (their docs live there). You can resend reminders in one click.
"I already use a spreadsheet."
Spreadsheets don't catch a policy that lapses mid-year. They don't remind your sub 30 days before expiration. They don't alert you the day your audit starts. One missed expiry date costs more than 17 years of SubDocs.
"I've never had a problem."
Most GCs don't know they had a problem until the audit bill arrives. By then it's too late to produce retroactive coverage. SubDocs is your proof that you did everything right.
"Is this like myCOI?"
myCOI requires 200 certificates minimum and charges enterprise prices with a mandatory sales call. SubDocs is built for GCs managing 5–30 subs, at $29/month, with a 14-day free trial. No sales call needed — sign up in 3 minutes.
