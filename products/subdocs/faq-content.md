## 25 Q&As for website FAQ + support. GC language throughout.

## GETTING STARTED

### Q: What is SubDocs?
**A:** SubDocs collects COIs from your subs automatically, tracks expiry dates, and alerts you 30 days before anything runs out. No more spreadsheets. No more chasing subs for updated docs. Just add a sub, send them a link, and let it work.

### Q: How long does setup take?
**A:** 5 minutes. Sign up, add your first sub, copy the upload link. That's it. Your sub uploads docs on their own time — no login required on their end.

### Q: Do I have to train my subs on how to use SubDocs?
**A:** No. They just click the link, select a file, and upload. It's simpler than email. If they get stuck, they can text or email you, and you can resend the link anytime.

### Q: Can I import my existing sub list?
**A:** Not yet — that's coming in Month 2. For now, add subs manually (takes 2 minutes per sub) or email us and we'll import them for you if you're upgrading to Pro.

### Q: Is there a trial?
**A:** Yes. Free tier has no trial limit — you get 5 free subs forever. No credit card required. Upgrade to Pro ($29/mo) whenever you're ready for unlimited subs.

### Q: What if I have questions during setup?
**A:** Email Rishi directly: rishi@getsubdocs.com. He reads every message and responds same day.

## SUBCONTRACTORS & DOCUMENTS

### Q: What documents does SubDocs track?
**A:** By default, COI (Certificate of Insurance). Optional: W9, licenses, and other docs. You decide what's required for your subs.

### Q: Why is COI the default requirement?
**A:** Because it's the only document your insurance carrier checks at audit. W9 and license are nice-to-have but not audit-critical.

### Q: Can I require different documents for different subs?
**A:** Not yet. All subs get the same document requirements. Coming soon: customization per trade or per sub.

### Q: What file types and sizes does SubDocs accept?
**A:** PDF, JPG, PNG. Max 10MB per file. Most COIs are 1-2MB. If a file is larger, ask the sub to re-export as a lower resolution.

### Q: Can my sub upload multiple files?
**A:** Yes. One link, multiple uploads. Your sub can upload a COI, W9, and license in one session.

### Q: What happens if my sub uploads the wrong document?
**A:** You'll see it in the dashboard. You can mark it as rejected and ask the sub to re-upload. Just send them the same link again — it works multiple times until you generate a new one.

### Q: Can my sub see documents from other GCs they work for?
**A:** Yes — that's the SubDocs sub portal. They can see all their docs across all GCs they work for, organized by company. Helps them stay on top of expirations.

### Q: How long does a sub have to upload?
**A:** Upload links expire after 30 days. If your sub hasn't uploaded by then, generate a new link and resend it. No problem.

### Q: Can I upload documents on behalf of my subs?
**A:** Yes. You can upload directly to the dashboard. We'll note that the GC uploaded it, not the sub.

## COMPLIANCE STATUS & ALERTS

### Q: What do the green/yellow/red/pending badges mean?
**A:**
- **Green (Compliant):** COI on file, not expiring within 30 days. ✓
- **Yellow (Expiring Soon):** COI on file, but expires within 30 days. ⚠️
- **Red (Non-Compliant):** COI expired, missing, or invalid. ✗
- **Pending:** No docs uploaded yet. —

### Q: When does the status update after a sub uploads?
**A:** Instantly. Dashboard refreshes in real-time.

### Q: What does "expiring soon" mean exactly?
**A:** Within 30 days of today. So if a COI expires in 25 days, you're in yellow.

### Q: Do I get alerts when a sub's COI is expiring?
**A:** Yes. You get an email 30 days before, 14 days before, and 7 days before expiry. The sub also gets emails at the same intervals. On Pro tier, you can enable SMS alerts too.

### Q: Can I turn off alerts?
**A:** Yes. In Settings → Notifications, toggle email/SMS on or off. But we recommend keeping 30-day alerts on for audit prep.

### Q: What if my sub's COI expires and they haven't renewed it yet?
**A:** You'll see red status. Dashboard shows exactly which doc is expired. Send a reminder link via email/SMS, and your sub can upload the updated COI when they have it.

### Q: Can I see which subs are expiring across all my projects?
**A:** Yes. Dashboard has an "Expiring Soon" section that shows all subs across all projects where docs expire within 30 days.

### Q: How do I know if I'm audit-ready?
**A:** All your subs should be green (or at least yellow, not red). Run the "Export Compliance Report" from your dashboard 60 days before your audit to double-check.

## BILLING & PRICING

### Q: How much does SubDocs cost?
**A:** Free (5 subs), Pro ($29/month, unlimited subs), Business ($79/month, Pro + SMS + multi-user).

### Q: Can I pay annually?
**A:** Yes. Pro is $290/year (save $58), Business is $790/year (save $158).

### Q: What's the difference between Pro and Business?
**A:** Pro: email reminders, unlimited subs. Business: email + SMS reminders, multi-user access, compliance reports, dedicated support.

### Q: When do I get charged?
**A:** On signup day for your first month. Then monthly or annually depending on your billing preference.

### Q: Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?
**A:** Yes. Upgrade instantly (no waiting). Downgrade takes effect at the end of your current billing cycle (no refunds for partial months).

### Q: What if I downgrade from Pro to Free but have more than 5 subs?
**A:** Extra subs get archived (hidden, not deleted). Upgrade back to Pro anytime to reactivate them. Your data is always safe.

### Q: Do you offer refunds?
**A:** If you upgrade and change your mind within 7 days, we'll refund the difference. Email rishi@getsubdocs.com with "refund request" and we'll sort it out same day.

### Q: What if I only use free forever — is that okay?
**A:** Totally fine. Free tier is unlimited in time. The 5-sub limit is the only constraint. No questions asked.

### Q: Can I get a discount for annual commitment?
**A:** Yes — see annual pricing above. 2 months free compared to monthly.

## SECURITY & PRIVACY

### Q: Where are my documents stored?
**A:** Supabase cloud storage (AWS data centers, US regions). Encrypted at rest and in transit (HTTPS). Industry-standard security.

### Q: Who can see my documents?
**A:** Only you (the GC) and the specific sub who uploaded them. Your other subs can't see each other's docs. No one else has access.

### Q: Is my data encrypted?
**A:** Yes. All data in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Documents are encrypted and tied to your account via row-level security.

### Q: Do you sell my data?
**A:** No. We don't sell data, period. We don't share it with insurance carriers, auditors, or anyone else without explicit permission.

### Q: What happens if I cancel my account?
**A:** Your data is deleted after 30 days. If you change your mind, email rishi@getsubdocs.com within 30 days and we'll restore it.

### Q: Can multiple people on my team log in?
**A:** Free and Pro: one user (the owner). Business tier: invite up to 3 team members (office manager, project manager, etc.).

### Q: What's your privacy policy?
**A:** Available at getsubdocs.com/privacy. TL;DR: we collect minimal data, don't sell it, and use standard encryption.

## TROUBLESHOOTING

### Q: My sub says the upload link doesn't work.
**A:**
1. Check if the link is expired (30-day limit). If so, generate a new one.
2. Try a different browser (Safari sometimes has issues).
3. On phone, try opening in incognito mode.
4. Have them email you the error message and we'll debug it.

### Q: I sent an upload link but my sub never uploaded. What now?
**A:** Generate a fresh link and resend via email or text. Old links still work but resending is easiest. If they still don't upload after 7 days, reach out to them directly.

### Q: My sub uploaded the wrong document type.
**A:** You'll see it in the dashboard. Mark it as "reject" and ask the sub to re-upload the correct doc. Send them the same link — it works until you generate a new one.

### Q: The expiry date my sub entered is wrong.
**A:** You can edit it manually in the dashboard. Just click the document row and update the date.

### Q: I lost my password. How do I reset it?
**A:** Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. Check your email for a reset link. (Check spam folder too.)

### Q: I'm not getting email alerts.
**A:** Check Settings → Notifications (might be toggled off). Check your spam folder. If still missing, email rishi@getsubdocs.com.

### Q: Can I import COIs from an old system or spreadsheet?
**A:** Not yet. We're building CSV import in Month 2. For now, email us the spreadsheet and we'll help you migrate if you upgrade to Pro.

### Q: What's the difference between "sent by sub" and "sent by GC" on a document?
**A:** "Sent by sub" = they uploaded via their link. "Sent by GC" = you uploaded on their behalf. Both are valid.

### Q: Can I delete a sub or document?
**A:** You can archive a sub (they disappear from your roster but data stays). Documents don't get deleted—they stay for your records. If you need to permanently delete something, email rishi@getsubdocs.com.
