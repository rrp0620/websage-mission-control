## MESSAGING CHECKLIST

When writing any follow-up email, make sure:

- [ ] Subject line has the GC's pain or curiosity (not generic)
- [ ] Under 150 words (mobile-friendly, busy contractors don't read essays)
- [ ] One clear call-to-action (button or link)
- [ ] Signed by Rishi (builds founder-led trust)
- [ ] No "vendor language" (no "compliance," "leverage," "utilize")
- [ ] References the ROI math or audit fear where possible
- [ ] Mobile-optimized (no fancy formatting, text-based)
- [ ] No images (faster load, works in all email clients)
